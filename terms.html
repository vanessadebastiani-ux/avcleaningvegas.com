SMS Terms

Effective October 20, 2026

Program description

AV Cleaning LV sends text messages to clients and prospective clients who opt in. Messages include estimate scheduling, appointment reminders (for example, "We'll see you tomorrow for your cleaning"), on-the-way and running-late updates on the day of service, and payment reminders. We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

How to opt in

You can opt in by checking the text message consent box on the estimate request form at avcleaningvegas.com, or by telling us verbally or in writing that you agree to receive text messages. Consent is not a condition of service.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on your cleaning schedule. Message and data rates may apply.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again. You can also contact us using the details below.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (702) 210-8380 or info@avcleaningvegas.com.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We do not share or sell your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
