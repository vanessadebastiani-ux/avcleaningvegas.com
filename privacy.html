Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

AV Cleaning LV ("AV Cleaning," "we," "us") provides residential cleaning services in Las Vegas and Henderson, Nevada. This policy explains what information we collect through avcleaningvegas.com and our text messages, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us when you request an estimate, call, text or email us: your name, phone number, email address, home address and ZIP code, details about your home (such as bedrooms and bathrooms), the services you want, and any notes you add.
  • Text message consent: whether you agreed to receive text messages from us, and when.
  • Service records: your cleaning schedule, visit notes and payment status.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your estimate and schedule your cleanings.
  • To send the text messages you agreed to receive: appointment reminders, on-the-way and running-late updates, and payment reminders.
  • To provide, manage and improve our services and answer your questions.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as our database, website hosting and text messaging providers, and only so they can perform those services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Text messaging

If you opt in, we will text you about your estimate and cleanings. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms for details.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. We keep it only as long as we need it to provide our services or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop receiving text messages, by contacting us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
